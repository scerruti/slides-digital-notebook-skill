# Posting an internship
1. Click **New posting** on your dashboard.
2. Fill in the title, dates, hours per week, location (on-site, remote, hybrid) and whether it's paid.
3. Write 3-5 sentences on what the scholar will do and learn.
4. Choose which schools can see the posting, or "All partner schools".
5. Click **Submit for review**. Postings are reviewed within 1 business day.

Tip: postings that list a specific project get twice as many applications.
