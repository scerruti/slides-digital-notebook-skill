# Getting started as an employer partner

Welcome! Starteryou connects your organization with high school scholars looking for internships.

## Create your account
Go to the [employer sign-up page](https://example.com/employer/signup) and enter your work email. You'll get a confirmation email within a few minutes. Click the link to confirm.

## Build your company profile
Add your logo, a two-sentence description, your address and your industry. Scholars see this profile before they apply, so keep it friendly and specific. Profiles with a logo get more applicants.

## Get verified
Before you can post, our team verifies your organization. Upload a document showing your organization is registered (business license, 501(c)(3) letter or similar) on the [verification page](https://example.com/employer/verify). Verification takes 2-3 business days. You'll get an email when you're approved.

## Set up your team
You can invite colleagues to help review applicants. Go to Settings > Team and add their emails. Each person gets their own login.

## Background checks
Any staff member who supervises a minor must complete a background check. See the hiring section for details.
